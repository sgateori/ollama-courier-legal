Ollama Courier Privacy Policy

App: Ollama Courier (com.ollamacourier) · Developer: sgateori · Last updated: 2026-10-22

Ollama Courier downloads Ollama models onto your phone (including an SD card), carries them, and pushes them to a model registry you run, or lets your computer pull them from the phone. It has no accounts, shows no ads and sells no data. The developer runs no server for this app and does not receive your models. This page says what stays on your phone and what leaves it.

What stays on your device

Your downloaded models, the model catalog, the registry addresses and bridge token you enter, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage and in app-specific storage (internal or SD card). The developer cannot see them. Android cloud backup is turned off for the app. Export and import use Android's folder picker. Uninstalling the app deletes its stored data.

Where your models and connections go

  • Downloads: models are downloaded from the registry address set in Settings, by default https://registry.ollama.ai. That server sees your phone's IP address and the model you request. It is a third party; its policy applies.
  • Push to your registry: model files and manifests are sent to the registry host you type in (over your LAN, Tailscale or WireGuard), using http or https as you enter it. If you set a bridge token, it is sent to that bridge in a header. The destination is your own server; the developer receives nothing. The app allows plain http for these connections, so traffic to a host you configure with http is not encrypted.
  • Send to Ollama / over USB: while you share a model, the app runs a read-only registry server on your phone. It accepts only Wi-Fi, Ethernet, VPN or USB-tether peers, requires a random token in the address, stops itself after idle, finish or too many wrong tokens, and uses plain http. Your computer pulls the files from the phone and can also fetch a generated import script. Nothing goes to the internet or the developer.
  • Pre-filled addresses: a fresh install pre-fills the home-registry settings with two private example addresses (one local-network, one Tailscale). The app may try to connect to them until you change them. Those attempts are not sent to the developer.

The app can check whether the Tailscale and WireGuard apps are installed, only to offer to open them.

Crash reports and anonymous usage (Firebase)

To fix crashes and see which features matter, the app uses Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics. Google acts as a service provider processing this data for us.

This is on by default. There is no question at first launch: collection starts when the app first opens, before you can reach the setting. You can turn it off at any time with the switch Share crash reports and usage statistics in Settings, Privacy, which stops collection by both services. It is always off in debug builds.

The app is designed not to send model names, registry addresses or hosts, your bridge token, file names or network names, and it does not use the advertising ID.

Limit: this filtering is not complete. The app removes the message from the handled problems it reports itself, and its log lines pass through a filter that removes web addresses, host and port pairs, digests and the model and host of the current transfer, before they are attached to reports as breadcrumbs. But if the app crashes with an uncaught error, the error text written by the system or a library is sent as it is, and some log lines are not filtered. In rare cases the text sent might include a model name, a local file path (an SD card path includes the volume ID) or a host name.

Google may see your phone's IP address when the upload happens and may derive an approximate country or region from it. The app itself never reads your location.

Data is sent over HTTPS. Retention is set by Google's defaults for Firebase. See Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your model files, registry addresses, bridge token, file names, location, contacts, advertising ID, or any account or personal information. We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

Permissions

Internet and network state (downloads, pushes, and the Wi-Fi-only check); Wi-Fi state (Wi-Fi and LAN handling around sharing); notifications (transfer progress and sharing status); foreground services of the data-sync type (keep a long download, push, export, import or sharing session running with the screen off); wake lock (held only while transfers or sharing run); and a receive-boot-completed permission added by the background-work library used for resuming queued transfers. No storage, location, contacts, camera or microphone access, and no all-files access.

Your choices and deleting data

Use the switch above to stop collection at any time. Delete models in the app, or uninstall to remove all local data. Wi-Fi-only transfers are the default. We have no account system, so we cannot look up your data by name. To ask us to delete reports tied to your Firebase installation ID, email us that ID or a description of when a crash happened, and we will delete what we can match. We cannot delete data held by a registry you downloaded from or pushed to.

Children

Ollama Courier is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this changes, the date above changes and the app update notes will say so.
